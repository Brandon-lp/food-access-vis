07 Food sales & income

Paychecks and
food bills.

Since 1997, both income and food sales per person have risen in every state. In 43 of 51 (the 50 states plus D.C.), income grew faster.

Pick a state on the map to watch how the two changed from 1997 to 2025, or switch views to see food sales as a share of income.

Sources: U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Economic Research Service (ERS), per capita food sales by state (total food); U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA), Annual Personal Income by State. 1997–2025, in nominal dollars (not adjusted for inflation). Food sales count food sold in a state divided by its residents, so places with many visitors or commuters, such as D.C., Nevada and Hawaii, can look higher than what residents spend.
