Sources: U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Economic Research Service (ERS), per capita food sales by state (total food); U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA), Annual Personal Income by State. 1997–2025, in nominal dollars (not adjusted for inflation). The U.S. average is the simple average of the 50 states and D.C., not weighted by population. Food sales count food sold in a state divided by its residents, so places with many visitors or commuters, such as D.C., Nevada and Hawaii, can look higher than what residents spend.
