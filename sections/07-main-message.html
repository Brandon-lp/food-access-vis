06 Main message

SNAP helps.
It isn't enough.

The U.S. is trying to help: in 2024, about 1 in 8 U.S. households (11.8%) received SNAP food benefits.

But the help has not kept up. Between 2011 and 2019, what an average American spends on food each month rose by about a third, while the average SNAP benefit went down. By 2019 it covered less than a third of a month's food.
