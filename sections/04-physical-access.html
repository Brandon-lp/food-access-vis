03 Physical access

A flat national number
hides big local shifts.

The first dimension of food access is physical: can you get to a store? The USDA counts people as having low access when they live more than 1 mile from a supermarket in a city, or more than 10 miles away in the countryside.

Between 2015 and 2019, that number fell by only about 1% nationwide. Underneath that flat total, places moved in opposite directions. Access improved in 32 states and worsened in 19. Across counties the split was almost even, and more than 1,200 counties shifted by over 10% one way or the other.
