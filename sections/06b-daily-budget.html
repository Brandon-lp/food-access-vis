What can $1.41 of food benefits
buy in a day?

Where this number comes from

Each month, the program spends a set amount on food for every person it helps. We split that amount across the days of the month, using 30.4, the average number of days in a month, to see what is spent on one person in one day.

Food assistance programs help families put meals on the table. Across the U.S., they spend an average of $1.41 on food per participant per day.

Pick a state, then set its coin next to the price of everyday items. Hover over (or tap) the highlighted number to see how it is worked out.

  • One full picture = the coin buys that much
  • A part-filled picture = the coin covers only part of one
