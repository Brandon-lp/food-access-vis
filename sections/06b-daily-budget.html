05b Daily budget

$1.41 a day,
what does it buy?

Divide what an agency spends per participant each month by the days in the month and you get one daily coin. Across 90 state, territory and tribal agencies, the average is $1.41 per participant per day.

Pick a place, then set its coin next to the price of everyday items. A part-filled picture means the coin covers only part of one.

  • One full picture = the coin buys that much
  • A part-filled picture = the coin covers only part of one

Sources: program spending and participants by agency, October 2015 to September 2016, from the project's three data files; per day = per month divided by 30.4. Item prices are national averages from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (Average Retail Food Prices, same 12 months). The transit ride is the 2014 national average from the Bureau of Transportation Statistics, so it is not from the same period. Prices are not what any program pays, and the comparison shows scale, not what is enough.
