01 Context

Food access is
not evenly mapped.

Across the country, more than 19 million people live in low-income areas far from a supermarket. The average distance is not just a number. It is time, transit, weather, and the weight of a week of groceries.

This project uses a sample of counties to make that unevenness visible. The chart below is designed as a starting point for your own investigation.
